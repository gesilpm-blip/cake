## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an artisanal patisserie and luxury confectionery experience. The aesthetic balances heritage culinary craftsmanship with contemporary editorial luxury. It speaks to discerning epicures, boutique shoppers, and gift seekers who appreciate exquisite ingredients, tactile textures, and culinary mastery.

The visual direction combines **Tactile Warmth** and **High-Contrast Editorial Elegance**:
- Decadent, saturated finishes reminiscent of melted ganache, spun sugar, and dusted cocoa.
- Lavish typography pairing a literary, high-contrast serif display with clean, humanist structural sans for effortless legibility.
- Generous breathing space layered with delicate, whisper-thin keylines and rich chocolate borders.
- Tangible, multi-layered surfaces evoking buttery parchment, glazed glazes, and velvet crumb textures.

## Colors

The color architecture is rooted in pure confectionery gastronomy:

- **Primary (`#2B1410` — Deep Bittersweet Cacao):** An ultra-rich, decadent 85% cocoa dark tone. Serves as the primary structural color for high-contrast titles, primary interactive buttons, and authoritative borders.
- **Secondary (`#D48B28` — Golden Brioche):** A rich, roasted butter and honey crust tone that radiates oven warmth. Deployed for accent badges, key highlights, active state glows, and luxury focal points.
- **Tertiary (`#A8324E` — Velvet Raspberry Rosewood):** A deep, saturated berry velvet providing punchy contrast and appetizing energy without descending into washed-out pastel tones. Used for promotion pills, primary calls-to-action that require urgency, seasonal badges, and heart actions.
- **Neutral (`#FDF8F3` — Clotted Cream & Warm Biscuit):** A buttery, warm porcelain base. It removes the sterile chill of pure white, imparting the welcoming feeling of floured marble pastry slabs and warm baker's parchment.
- **Surface Accents:**
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#F5EBE1` (Warm Oat Biscuit) for container cards and input backdrops.
  - `Accent Cream`: `#FCE8EE` (Strawberry Cream infusion) for subtle notification tints, tertiary hover states, and discount chips.

## Typography

The typographical pairing strikes an intentional tension between classical culinary romance and modern tactile utility.

- **Headline & Display (`Playfair Display`):** Evokes copper-plate menus, gilded salon signage, and classical European recipe volumes. Headlines utilize tighter negative tracking and deliberate line-height to project confidence and sumptuous quality.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its friendly geometric warmth, generous x-height, and open apertures. Keeps ingredient listings, dietary allergen pills, and checkout order summaries crisp and instantly scannable without competing with the ornate display headings.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop) centered within a maximum width container of `1280px`.

- **Mobile (< 768px):** Outer canvas margins collapse to `margin-mobile` (`1.25rem`) with `1rem` column gutters. Vertical spacing relies on `space-md` between elements and `space-xl` between sections to preserve an unhurried, boutique ambiance.
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** Generous `3rem` margins frame the layout, reminiscent of the wide borders of a luxury cookbook. Multi-column editorial cards allow showcase bakery photography to breathe against warm biscuit negative space.
- **Micro Spacing:** Internal component padding scales strictly along the `space-*` scale to maintain rhythm across cards, dropdown panels, and checkout forms.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons clinical grey drop-shadows in favor of **Cacao-Tinted Ambient Warmth** and **Layered Patisserie Surfaces**:

- **Tonal Layering:** 
  - Canvas base rests at `#FDF8F3` (Clotted Cream).
  - Raised cards, sheets, and pastry showcases sit on `#FFFFFF` or `#F5EBE1` (Warm Biscuit).
- **Ambient Warm Shadows:** Shadows are warm, soft, and infused with deep chocolate undertones:
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(43, 20, 16, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 20, 16, 0.04)`
  - *Floating Modals / Flyout Cart:* `0 20px 36px -4px rgba(43, 20, 16, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -2px rgba(43, 20, 16, 0.06)`
- **Crumb Keylines:** Rather than harsh CSS borders, containment employs delicate hairline strokes (`1px solid rgba(43, 20, 16, 0.08)`) to structure content with the precision of fine pastry scoring.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature. Elements possess an organic, baked softness—reminiscent of plump brioche buns, rounded tart shells, and sculpted chocolate bars—without being childishly circular.

- Standard buttons and text fields use `0.5rem` (8px).
- Product cards, modal containers, and hero carousels adopt `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Special badges, quantity steppers, and pill tags use `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) or full circular pills for a tactile, touchable appeal.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Deep Bittersweet Cacao):** Background `#2B1410`, text `#FDF8F3`. On hover, subtle lift with background shifting to a slightly warmer roast tone (`#3D1E18`) and warm chocolate shadow.
- **Secondary (Velvet Raspberry Rosewood):** Background `#A8324E`, text `#FFFFFF`. Reserved for high-value impulse conversions like "Add to Baker's Box" or "Pre-order for Weekend".
- **Tertiary / Outline:** `1.5px` border in `#2B1410` over transparent or `#F5EBE1` background, with text in `#2B1410`.
- **Golden Accent Button:** Background `#D48B28`, text `#2B1410` (bold), used sparingly for VIP tasting clubs or limited holiday editions.

### Product & Confectionery Cards
- Base in `#FFFFFF` with `1px` border in `rgba(43, 20, 16, 0.08)` and subtle cacao ambient shadow.
- High-contrast typography layout: Item title set in `Playfair Display` with pricing displayed in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans` in `#D48B28`.
- Image frames incorporate soft `rounded-lg` top radius with subtle cream inset highlights.

### Chips & Flavor Tags
- Compact tags highlighting tasting notes (e.g., "Madagascar Vanilla", "Tart Raspberry", "Sourdough Crumb").
- Rendered in `#FCE8EE` (Strawberry Cream) background with `#A8324E` (Raspberry Velvet) text, or `#F5EBE1` background with `#2B1410` text. All shaped with full-radius pills (`rounded-xl`).

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Fields use an organic biscuit-tinted fill (`#F5EBE1` at 60% opacity) with a `1px` boundary in `rgba(43, 20, 16, 0.15)`.
- Active focus state: Ring halo of `2px` in `#D48B28` (Golden Brioche) with border shifting to `#2B1410`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square-rounded boxes (`4px` radius) and rounded radios. 
- In active state, filled with `#2B1410` housing a `#FDF8F3` crisp checkmark or dot.

### Specialized Bakery Components
- **Flour-Dust Flavor Ribbons:** Diagonal or pinned ribbon labels on pastry cards using `#A8324E` with uppercase `label-sm` text.
- **Allergen & Dietary Badges:** Minimalist circular tokens with clear iconographic emblems (Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut-Free) bordered in `#D48B28`.
- **Baker's Quantity Counter:** Segmented pill container (`#F5EBE1`) with deep cacao increment/decrement triggers and bold central count.
